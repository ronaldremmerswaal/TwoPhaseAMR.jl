\chapter{Block-tree AMR mesh}\label{ch:mesh-amr}
This chapter will describe tree topology and schedules for a graded quadtree.
Thesis Chapter~2 introduces graded refinement and the mesh-width hierarchy,
but does not describe this package's block-tree implementation~\cite{remmerswaal2023}.

\section{Blocks, levels, and grading}
Let $\tree$ be the block tree, $\leafset$ its leaf set, $\mathcal{B}$ a block,
and $\level$ the maximum refinement level. The spacing at level $\ell$ is
$2^{-\ell}h_0$.
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{BlockTreeMesh}, \apitype{BlockKey}, \apitype{LeafBlock}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Ownership and schedules}
The leaf-cell and face schedules, including hanging segments $\hangsegment{f}$,
are the units of mesh traversal.
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{CellRef}, \apitype{FaceRef}, \apifun{isbalanced}.
\end{implnote}
