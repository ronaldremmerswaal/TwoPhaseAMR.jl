interpolants~\cite{remmerswaal2023,remmerswaal2022}. This chapter will map
staggered transport, its $\massflux$ fluxes, limiters, and AMR schedules.

\section{Flux interpolants and limiters}
The implemented interpolants include Lax--Wendroff and Sweby-form limiters;
see Sweby~\cite{sweby1984} and Zeng~\cite{zeng2016}.
\begin{deviation}{Directional momentum advection}
The package alternates directional sweeps with a variable-velocity Lax--Wendroff predictor; the thesis interpolants use unsplit donating-region centroids.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{LaxWendroff}, \apitype{MonotonizedCentral}, \apitype{VanLeer},
\apifun{advect!}.
\end{implnote}
