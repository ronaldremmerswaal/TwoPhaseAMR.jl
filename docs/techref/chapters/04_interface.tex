\chapter{Interface description and curvature}\label{ch:interface}
Thesis Chapters~2--5 cover the interface, reconstruction, and curvature
including local and geometric height functions~\cite{remmerswaal2023}.
This chapter will map the implemented PLIC representation, initialisation,
curvature fallbacks, and contact angles.

\section{Fractions and reconstruction}
The liquid and gas fractions are denoted $\volfrac^\liq$ and $\volfrac^\gas$.
\begin{deviation}{Reconstruction family}
Optimisation-based MOF/LVIRA and parabolic PLVIRA/PROST/PMOF reconstructions from the thesis are not implemented.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{InterfaceField}, \apifun{reconstruct!}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Interface initialisation}
Level-set quadrature and exact polygon clipping initialise the phase fraction;
see also Saye~\cite{saye2015} and Kromer and Bothe~\cite{kromer2021}.
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{PolygonIndicator}, \apifun{initialize!}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Height functions and contact angle}
Interface curvature $\kappa$ uses local height functions with configured
geometric-height-function or WY fallbacks and boundary contact angles.
\begin{deviation}{Local height-function quality}
The implemented LHF quality weight uses the approved smoothstep correction to the thesis's printed polynomial.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{InterfaceField}, \apifun{interface_height}; internal
\texttt{\detokenize{_height_quality}}.
\end{implnote}
