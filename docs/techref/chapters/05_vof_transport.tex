\chapter{Geometric VOF transport}\label{ch:vof-transport}
Thesis Chapters~3 and~6 describe geometric VOF transport and one- and
two-velocity mass fluxes~\cite{remmerswaal2023}. This chapter will specify
the directional Weymouth--Yue (WY) implementation~\cite{weymouth2010}, its
carriers, CFL restrictions, and fraction handling.

\section{Directional WY sweeps}
The indicator $\chi$ is frozen during the split update, with PLIC
reconstruction between sweeps.
\begin{deviation}{Split donating regions}
The thesis's two-fluid scheme uses unsplit donating regions; the package uses directional WY sweeps.
\end{deviation}
\begin{deviation}{First-moment transport}
Unlike the thesis's centroid-advection formulation, no centroid or first moment is advected.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{WYAdvectionWorkspace}, \apifun{advect!}, \apifun{stable_timestep}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Fraction bounds and CFL}
The WY limit $\wycflval$ and incoming, outgoing, and extrusion bounds will be
stated alongside the implementation's fraction-floor policy.
\begin{deviation}{Fraction snapping}
Clamping or snapping fractions at the configured floor is non-conservative.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{InterfaceField}, \apifun{stable_timestep}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Two-velocity interface carriers}
The frozen and projected-liquid policies determine phase transport; their
sweep coupling will be documented here.
\begin{deviation}{Projected-liquid carrier}
The projected-liquid policy and its split-sweep auxiliary gas reset are not in the thesis.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{FrozenInterfaceFluxes}, \apitype{ProjectedLiquidVelocity},
\apifun{phase_flux_diagnostics}.
\end{implnote}
