\chapter{Stability and accuracy}\label{ch:stability-accuracy}
The thesis describes transport CFL limits and capillary and gravity time-step
bounds in Chapters~3, 4, and~7~\cite{remmerswaal2023}. This chapter will
collect the implemented limits, their defaults, and the known order restrictions.

\section{Advective and capillary time steps}
Limits for staggered transport, WY sweeps, and capillary waves will be
expressed using the minimum mesh width $h$ and time step $\dt$.
\begin{implnote}
\apifun{stable_timestep}, \apitype{AdvectionOptions}, \apitype{SurfaceTension}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Gravity time-step limit}
The gravity bound depends on the phase densities $\rho^\liq,\rho^\gas$ and
$\abs{\+g}$.
\begin{deviation}{Gravity time-step factor}
The implemented bound contains an extra factor $1/2$ inside the square root and is therefore $\sqrt{2}$ stricter than the thesis's gravity bound.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apifun{stable_timestep}, \apitype{TwoPhase}.
\end{implnote}
