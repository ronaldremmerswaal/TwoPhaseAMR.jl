\chapter{Method overview}\label{ch:overview}
\chapterstatus{outline}
This chapter will map the incompressible one- and two-velocity models and a
complete time step. Thesis Chapters~2 and~7 describe the governing equations
and two-phase step ordering~\cite{remmerswaal2023}; the order of the complete
single-phase discretisation requires a separate analysis.

\section{Models and scope}
The implemented geometries are two-dimensional; the physical models are
incompressible.
\begin{deviation}{Dimensionality and compressibility}
The thesis also treats three dimensions and compressible extensions; neither is implemented here.
\end{deviation}
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{SinglePhase}, \apitype{TwoPhase}, \apitype{OneVelocityModel},
\apitype{TwoVelocityModel}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Time-step stages}
The thesis notation uses $\velo^{\stage{1}}$ after transport, $\velo^{\stage{2}}$ after diffusion, and
$\velo^{\stage{3}}$ after forces, followed by projection. Body forces and boundary
updates are also part of the implementation sequence.
\begin{implnote}
\apitype{FlowSimulation}, \apifun{step!}, \apifun{simulate!}.
\end{implnote}

\section{Accuracy and model limits}
The formal order depends on the model and splitting; no global second-order
claim is made in this outline. See Chapter~\ref{ch:stability-accuracy}.
