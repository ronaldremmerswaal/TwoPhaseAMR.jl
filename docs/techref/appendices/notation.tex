\chapter{Notation}\label{app:notation}
The notation is that of the thesis~\cite{remmerswaal2023}, extended for the
block forest, owned face segments and halo slots. Every symbol is a macro of
\code{techref-notation.sty}, and this table is generated from those
definitions, so it lists exactly the notation of this reference. The column
\emph{Thesis} gives the section or equation of the thesis that introduces a
symbol (\emph{nomencl.}: the thesis' nomenclature; \emph{new}: introduced
here). Groups marked with chapter numbers collect the notation introduced
there; those of chapters that are still outlines are provisional. Bound index
letters ($c$, $f$, $k$, $t$, $m$, $n$, \dots) are not listed. A square
$\square$ stands for any quantity.
