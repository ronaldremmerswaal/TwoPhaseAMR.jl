## Statement and assumptions

Claims audited (line numbers of the chapter at `69e59bc`):

1. (l. 94-112) The selected step $\min\{\wycflval\minwidth/\transspeed,\minwidth/(2\capspeed)\}$ of a flat interface translated along itself (uniform mesh, no solid) exceeds the Denner–van Wachem speed-corrected bound $\minwidth/(\sqrt2\capspeed+\transspeed)$ by at most $\wycflval+2^{-1/2}$ ($1.16$ at $0.45$), attained at $\transspeed=2\wycflval\capspeed$.
2. (l. 94-103) $\dtcap=\minwidth/(2\capspeed)$ with $\capspeed=(\pi\surftens/((\dens^{\liq}+\dens^{\gas})\minwidth))^{1/2}$, and $\dtcap=2^{-1/2}\times$ the period of the capillary wave of wavelength $\minwidth$.
3. (l. 75-80) $\dtcap\propto\minwidth^{3/2}$ becomes active, $\dtgrav\propto\minwidth^{1/2}$ inactive under refinement (advective bounds $\propto\minwidth$).
4. (l. 502-506) A first-order time error appears as order $1$, $3/2$, $1/2$ in $\minwidth$ when advection-, capillary-, gravity-limited.
5. (l. 207-212) The last step of a call lies in $[10^{-6},1+10^{-6})$ times its proposal whenever an earlier step of the same call preceded it.
6. (l. 188-203, 213) $\dtref=\max\{\dtprop,\dtdef,\dt\}$; the third term matters only for a stretched last step.
7. Spot checks: gravity bound $\sqrt2$ stricter than thesis (7.4) (`dev:stab:gravity`); `step!` accepts $(1+10^{-6}+32\varepsilon)\dtflow(\tfrac12)$ (`eq:stab:check`) and the "11 %" headroom $0.5/0.45$; the empirical margins ($2.5\times/2.75\times$ capillary, $1.35\times/1.5\times$ KH) reproduce from the script.

**Assumptions made explicit.** (a) $\rho=\dens^{\liq}+\dens^{\gas}$ in the thesis' $\langle\rho\rangle$ (confirmed by the dispersion relation in thesis `pt2_appendix.tex:68`); dispersion relations of the infinite-depth inviscid waves, $\omega^2=\surftens k^3/\rho$ and $\omega^2=gk|\Delta\rho|/\rho$. (b) Claim 1 uses $\dtwy=\wycflval\minwidth/\transspeed$ for a uniform velocity $(\transspeed,0)$ ($\cflout=\cflin=\cflext=\transspeed\dt/\minwidth$ in `eq:vof:cfl`, `src/vof/workspace.jl:272-309`); for a translation in a general direction the sum over both axes makes $\dtwy$ smaller by $|\cos\theta|+|\sin\theta|\ge1$, so the axis-parallel case is the worst case. (c) Claim 3 is at fixed velocity scale $\transspeed$ (the thesis' mesh Froude argument). (d) Claim 4 is conditional on a first-order time error that dominates; the chapter itself says no order in time is claimed. (e) Claim 5 is stated for the exact arithmetic of the loop; the times are rounded, so the lower end holds up to rounding of $t$ (relative $\approx\varepsilon\max|t|/(10^{-6}\dtprop)$).

## Errors and consequences

**E1 (wording, l. 75-76).** "in one phase in $(0,1]$" is the range of `stable_timestep`, not of a flow: every step is checked at $\dtstag(\stagcfl=\tfrac12)$ (`src/single_phase/stepping.jl:18-20`), so a single-phase `simulate!` with $\flowcfl>\tfrac12$ stops at its first advection-limited step (reproduced, `C5`). Replacement:

```latex
In two phases $\flowcfl$ must lie in $(0,\half]$. In one phase
\apifun{stable_timestep} accepts $(0,1]$, but \apifun{step!} checks every step
against $\dtstag$ at $\stagcfl=\half$~\cref{eq:stab:check}, so
\apifun{simulate!} with $\flowcfl>\half$ stops at its first advection-limited
step there too.
```

**E2 (l. 193-194).** The loop threshold is fixed at the start of the call. Replacement:
`...16\machineeps\max\{1,\abs{\tend},\abs{\timevar^{\timelevel{0}}}\}` (consequence only when $\timevar^{\timelevel{0}}<-\max\{1,\abs{\tend}\}$).

**E3 (cross-chapter, not in Chapter 13).** Chapter 13 states the check with $(1+10^{-6}+32\varepsilon)$ (code), but `05_vof_transport.tex:412-413, 419-420`, `06_momentum_transport.tex:656` and `08_forces.tex:747-748` still print $(1+32\machineeps)$ for the same checks of `advect!` and `step!`. Replace the factor by `(1+10^{-6}+32\machineeps)` and add `\cref{eq:stab:check}` (issue 115 is cited only in Chapter 13).

**E4 (already fixed at `69e59bc`).** "$\dtref=\dtprop$" for a shortened last step: the reference is $\max\{\dtprop,\dtdef,\dt\}\ge\dtprop$; it equals $\dtdef>\dtprop$ when the proposal fell. Verified in D2 (reference exceeds the proposal in the falling-bound run).

No error in the six requested claims; the Chapter 13 numbers are unchanged by the findings above.
